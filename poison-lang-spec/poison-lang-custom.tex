    \section{Semantics}%
    The operational semantics is given by a big-step evaluation relation.
    A configuration $\langle \pi, e, \mu, \rho \rangle$ evaluates to a
    result $\langle v, \mu', \rho' \rangle$, where $\pi$ is the purity
    mode, $e$ the expression, $\mu$ the memory, and $\rho$ the variable
    environment.\par\bigskip
